## James's welcome, inside the app

- First launch: [warm] Hi, I'm James. I'll be operating with you. Pick a case and meet me in the corridor.
- Back after a break: [casual] Good to see you again. Want to pick up where we left off?
- After a strong case: [warm] That was a good case. You took your time where it mattered.
- After a rough case: [calm] Not your best day, and that's fine. That's what this room is for. Let's look at it together.
